# PeMA voice library (approved)

**Status:** approved on 21 Nov 2026 by Claude under द्रष्टा (Drasta)'s instruction ("review as an expert meditation teacher, linguistic expert and inspirer, improve up to 3 rounds, avoid health claims, then approve"). Clinical and regulatory review is still outstanding; the owner is handling it.

Every line is recorded once in द्रष्टा (Drasta)'s voice the first time it is used, then played for every user. Claude can only choose and order these lines.

- **Lines:** 116
- **Total characters:** 4724 (about 4724 ElevenLabs credits if every line is ever recorded; estimate)
- **Pause** = seconds of silence after the line, played by the app (free)

## Review rounds

**Round 1 (teacher, linguist, inspirer):**
- Breathing pauses were too short to breathe (1 second after "breathe in for four"); every breathing line now leaves time to do it.
- Made lines work for everyone: lying down, wheelchair users, eyes closed, no sense of smell or taste.
- Softened commands into gentle invitations ("Let your jaw relax", "If it feels right...").
- Filled gaps: openings for feeling numb or very energetic, grounding purpose, "you can come back to your breath at any time", reflective and warm closings.
- Removed a physical-effect claim ("ease tension in your body" became "soften and let go").

**Round 2 (polish):** fixed four lines that only worked straight after another line or were vague (b02, k05, j04, c07).

**Round 3:** no meaningful changes, so the review stopped.

**Checked for every line:** one short sentence, everyday UK English, calm and secular, no health or medical claims, none of: journey, embrace, serenity, deep within, allow yourself.
